# Risks — Real Time Dating App

## High Risks

### Safety

Real-time dating can increase physical safety concerns.

Mitigation:

- verification
- public meeting suggestions
- safety check-ins
- reporting
- location privacy controls

### Trust

Dating apps suffer from fake profiles, scams, and low intent.

Mitigation:

- verification
- behavioral signals
- reputation system
- friction for suspicious actions

### Cold Start

Dating apps require network effects.

Mitigation:

- launch in one city/niche
- event-based entry
- community strategy
- invite/referral model

### Differentiation

Market is crowded.

Mitigation:

- choose a strong niche
- validate specific pain
- avoid generic dating app positioning

## Medium Risks

- moderation complexity
- privacy/legal requirements
- app store policy
- monetization too early
- gender ratio imbalance
