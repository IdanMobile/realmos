# Idea Brief — Real Time Dating App

## Raw Idea

A dating app that creates better real-time dating experiences using immediacy, context, and active intent.

## Core Hypothesis

Existing dating apps often feel slow, fake, swipe-heavy, and low-intent.

A product focused on real-time availability, honest intent, and immediate interaction may create a better experience for a specific niche.

## Early Differentiation Directions

- real-time availability
- date-now / meet-soon mode
- context-based matching
- intent verification
- safer in-app flow
- reduced endless swiping
- AI-assisted profile and conversation support
- location/time-aware experiences

## Immediate Questions

1. What is the exact niche?
2. What makes users trust the real-time experience?
3. How do we prevent unsafe meetups?
4. How do we avoid becoming just another dating app?
5. What is the MVP interaction loop?
